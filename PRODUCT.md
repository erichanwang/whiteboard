# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using Ubuntu who want a fast, local canvas for handwriting, diagrams, notes, and equations with a mouse, touch screen, stylus, or trackpad.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable native whiteboard that opens quickly, stays out of the way, saves work locally, and can optionally turn handwriting into editable text or LaTeX.

## Brand Personality

Clean, direct, and calm. The interface should feel like a well-made desktop tool rather than a web page or an AI demo.

## Anti-references

Avoid excessive glow, decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized controls, generic AI styling, and interactions that fall back to browser or operating-system prompts when an in-app control is appropriate.

## Design Principles

- Keep the canvas as the primary surface.
- Prefer familiar desktop-tool interactions and concise labels.
- Make local storage and privacy understandable without interrupting the workflow.
- Keep handwriting, text, images, and equations editable and reversible.
- Reuse the existing component and color vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast in whiteboard and blackboard modes, touch-sized controls, semantic labels, and reduced-motion behavior.
